## Intent

A launch short for SandBase Jev Trader: a market-making desk that quotes a bid or an
offer on a fictional ANTH/USD book every 300 ms tick, with an AI model choosing
the side and a live dashboard showing every decision, every order and the P&L.

The audience is the same crowd the original jev-trader project played to:
people who scroll past trading bots and stop for one that visibly acts several
times a second. The thing they have to feel in three seconds is the *rate*:
the tape never stops moving, and the chart is drawing itself in front of them.

Tone: instrument panel, not advertisement. Precise, quiet, a little cold. The
numbers are the design. Restraint over decoration, and no claim the simulation
cannot back up.

## Assets

- ../video/takes/hero.mp4 - 30 s native capture of the live dashboard at
  1920x1080; the visual source of truth for the running product.

## Customizations

- Numbers must be the real ones the run produced, never invented placeholders.
- The stand-in model badge and the "fictional listing" caveat are part of the

## Notes

- The traded company is private and the listing is fictional. No frame may
  imply a real Anthropic share price exists.
- Captions and on-screen type in English, matching the product UI.
- Music is a minimal low-frequency ambient bed, not a track with a hook.
- No blinking or pulsing status indicators.
